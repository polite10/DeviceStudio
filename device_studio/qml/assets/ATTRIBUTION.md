# Drive scene asset sources

Photographic sources are CC0 from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com); the
originals are not committed. `tools/generate_drive_assets.py --source DIR`
rebuilds the files below from them.

| Asset | Poly Haven source | Authors | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sky/sky_*.jpg`, `sky/probe.hdr` | [Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky) | Greg Zaal, Jarod Guest | CC0 |
| `surfaces/asphalt_*.jpg` | [Asphalt 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_02) | Rob Tuytel | CC0 |
| `surfaces/grass_*.jpg` | [Aerial Grass Rock](https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_grass_rock) | Rob Tuytel | CC0 |

The distant mountain ranges in the sky, foliage, concrete, terrain macro map,
contact shadow and sounds are procedural. Vehicle sources are listed in
`vehicles/README.md`.

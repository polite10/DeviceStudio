# Embedded 3D cars

Traffic cars: Quaternius Racing Kit, CC0. Original FBX and license are
preserved under `godot/adas_drive/assets/vehicles/quaternius/`. These meshes
are generated by `tools/import_qt_vehicles.py` with the installed PySide6
Balsam importer. Material bindings add clear-coated paint, tinted glass, lamps,
brake lamps, rotating wheels and body pitch/roll. Source centimetres are
converted to metres; source +Z is rotated toward the road's -Z at runtime.

`sahin/` and `model3/` are the selectable ego cars, original models of a boxy
1980s Turkish saloon and of a Tesla Model 3 style electric saloon, with no logos
or badges. `tools/sahin_blender.py` and `tools/model3_blender.py` build them in
Blender; `tools/build_sahin_model.py` and `tools/build_model3_model.py` convert
them with Balsam and write `Sahin.qml` and `Model3.qml`. Both also drive in traffic.

`roadster/` is the flying ego car, an original model of the 2028 Tesla Roadster
as revealed in October 2026, with no logos or badges. `tools/roadster_blender.py`
builds it and `tools/build_roadster_model.py` writes `Roadster.qml`, which adds
`thrust`, `boost` (cold gas nozzle glow and jets) and `wing` (pop-up tail wing)
to the shared car contract. It is not used in traffic.
